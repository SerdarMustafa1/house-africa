Who we are

A home for community,
culture, and connection

House Africa MTÜ is a non-profit organisation dedicated to fostering connection, inclusion, and mutual understanding among African communities and the wider diaspora in Estonia. Through shared cultural experiences, educational initiatives, and meaningful dialogue, the organisation creates spaces where people can connect, learn, and grow together.

What drives us

Our Core Pillars

Everything we do is rooted in three interconnected values.

Culture

Celebrating African identity, heritage, creativity, and shared traditions that enrich our communities.

Education

Promoting learning, awareness, and opportunities for communities to grow and thrive together.

Dialogue

Encouraging open conversations, inclusion, understanding, and collaboration across communities.

What we do

Building Community Together

We create meaningful spaces and experiences that bring people together.

🤝

Community Gatherings

Organising events and meetups where African communities and allies can come together, share stories, and build lasting connections.

🌍

Cultural Exchange

Creating opportunities for cultural sharing — from food and music to art and storytelling — that celebrate the richness of African heritage.

📚

Educational Activities

Supporting learning initiatives that raise awareness, build skills, and open doors for community members of all backgrounds.

🌱

Diaspora Support

Helping members of the African diaspora in Estonia feel seen, supported, and connected to both their roots and their new home.

💬

Inclusive Conversations

Facilitating open dialogue between communities, fostering mutual respect and a deeper understanding of shared experiences.

🤲

Collaborative Initiatives

Partnering with local organisations and individuals to build a more inclusive and vibrant society in Estonia.
